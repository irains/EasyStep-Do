# Product

## Register

product

## Users

EasyStep-Do is for individual knowledge workers who need a calm desktop todo tool for daily planning, lightweight task capture, and quick review. They use it during normal work hours and at the end of a week or month when they need to turn completed tasks into a report.

## Product Purpose

The product helps users keep dated tasks, Markdown details, completion state, and WebDAV sync in one compact desktop app. Success means the user can add tasks quickly, understand what belongs to today or a period, and produce a useful weekly or monthly work summary without rebuilding the list manually.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, clear, reliable. The app should feel focused and low-friction rather than decorative. It should support repeated daily use without visual fatigue.

## Anti-references

Avoid nested card stacks, SaaS dashboard hero metrics, noisy decoration, glassmorphism, heavy gradients, and interfaces that look like generic admin templates. Report views should not feel like a pile of KPI cards.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the workflow close to the task: adding, reviewing, and reporting should stay in the same mental space.
2. Prefer quiet hierarchy over decoration: use spacing, grouping, and type weight before adding boxes.
3. Make reports readable as working notes: summaries should look copy-ready, not like analytics dashboards.
4. Reduce nesting: one clear surface should usually be enough.
5. Keep repeated actions short: period switching and copying should be reachable without hunting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for accessible contrast, clear focus states, keyboard-reachable controls, and reduced reliance on color alone. Motion should be minimal and non-essential. Text should remain understandable in both Chinese and English.
